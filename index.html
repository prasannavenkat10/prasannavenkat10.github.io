03 · AI tools

Tools I built with AI

Each one started as a manual spreadsheet or WhatsApp process. I wrote the requirements, built it with Claude, and checked it against real chapter data before the team relied on it.

In use monthly

LTRT Seating & Registration

Problem
Seating at a 4-screen theatre was planned by hand in Excel, and registrations lived in a separate portal.
Built
A web app with seat maps from the venue's allotment plans, monthly editions, Joint LTRT mode for incoming and outgoing Head Tables, poster uploads and attendance tracking.
Outcome
Infinity Admin Portal exports are matched to invitees by name, with chapter as a tie-breaker. Unmatched or ambiguous names are listed, so nobody is missed.

Monthly scoring product

LTRT Chapter Scoresheet automation

Problem
Every month, 12 chapters in two regions are scored on 17 weighted parameters (150 points) by copying BNI Connect and PALMS reports into a manual workbook.
Built
A Google Sheets and Apps Script product with region-specific Business formulas, size-tiered induction targets, tie-aware ranking and colour-coded scorecards.
Outcome
One workbook that turns the month's source reports into ranked, colour-coded chapter scorecards. Validated chapter by chapter against the manual sheets, which also exposed formula errors in the old workbook. A 13-tab source template standardises data collection.
